So yesterday I finally fixed that leaky faucet. Took forever. My neighbor Dave, who apparently knows everything about plumbing (or so he claims), came over around six and just started poking at it with a wrench he pulled out of his truck. Didn't even ask if I had tools. Anyway, turns out it wasn't the washer, it was some weird corroded valve thing that I'd never even heard of before. We had to run to the hardware store twice because the first part didn't fit. Classic.

While we were there I ran into my old college roommate, Priya, who I hadn't seen in like three years. She's working in marketing now, somewhere downtown, and she looked exhausted but happy, which is a combination I feel like everyone has these days. We talked for maybe ten minutes in the parking lot about nothing in particular — her dog, my terrible attempt at sourdough bread last month, whether the new coffee place on 5th is actually any good (verdict: overpriced but the pastries are worth it).

By the time Dave and I got back it was almost eight and the light was fading fast. He held the flashlight while I tightened everything, cursing under my breath the whole time because my hands kept slipping. When it finally stopped dripping we just sat on the kitchen floor for a minute, both of us covered in grease, and he said "well, that's a story for the ages" which, honestly, it kind of is now.

I ordered pizza after. Too tired to cook. Dave left around nine, said something about needing to walk his dog before it got too late, and I just sat there scrolling through my phone doing absolutely nothing productive for another hour. Worth it though. No more drip at 3am waking me up.
