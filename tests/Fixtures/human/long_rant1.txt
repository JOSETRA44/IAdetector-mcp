Okay so I need to vent about my car for a second. It started making this noise last week — not a big noise, just this faint clicking thing whenever I turned left. Only left. Never right, which honestly made it worse somehow, like the car was being selective about when to betray me. I ignored it for four days because I'm an idiot and also because the mechanic near my apartment charges an absurd "diagnostic fee" just to look at it, which feels like a scam dressed up as a business model.

Anyway. Friday morning it got louder. Like, noticeably louder, to the point where my sister (who was in the passenger seat, half asleep, coffee in hand) sat straight up and said "what IS that." Great question, Rebecca. I have no idea. So we pulled into the first auto shop we saw, some place called Ace Motors that I'd never heard of, run by a guy named Tomas who turned out to be unreasonably nice about the whole thing.

He popped the hood, poked around for maybe ninety seconds, and just said "yeah, it's your CV joint, probably." Probably! I love that. Very reassuring. Turns out replacing a CV joint is not cheap, not quick, and apparently very common on cars like mine, which is a fact nobody tells you when you buy a used Honda with 140,000 miles on it for what felt like a great deal at the time.

We waited around for like two and a half hours. There was a vending machine that only took exact change, which of course neither of us had, so we just sat there being hungry and mildly annoyed, scrolling our phones, occasionally speculating about how much this was going to cost. Rebecca guessed four hundred. I guessed way more, because I am a pessimist and also because I've been burned before by mechanics who "find more things" once they've got the car up on the lift.

Turned out to be six hundred and change. Ouch. But hey, no more clicking, and Tomas threw in a free oil change because apparently mine was overdue by a couple thousand miles, which — fair, I hadn't checked. He also mentioned my tires were getting a little worn on the inside edge, alignment issue probably, but said it could wait a few months if I wasn't planning any road trips. I am not. Or at least I wasn't, until my cousin texted me an hour later asking if I wanted to drive down to visit her next month. Timing.

So now I'm sitting here doing math in my head about whether I should just get the alignment done now while I'm already out several hundred dollars, versus waiting and doing it separately later, versus just accepting that my car and my bank account are simply not going to get along this quarter. There's no elegant solution here. I think I just wait it out and complain about it to whoever will listen, which today happens to be whoever reads this.

On an unrelated note, my neighbor's cat has started showing up on my porch every evening around six, sits there for exactly ten minutes, then leaves. No idea whose cat it is. No collar. Rebecca thinks I should just adopt it by default since it clearly considers my porch part of its territory now. I'm not opposed, honestly, it's a good cat, very calm, doesn't meow much, just sort of observes. We've named it Gerald, unofficially, until someone tells us otherwise.

Anyway that's basically been my week. Car drama, mystery cat, an upcoming road trip I now have to plan around a possibly-still-slightly-off alignment. Living the dream over here, truly.
